Processing Facility & Quality Assurance

Dedicated Infrastructure

Headquartered in Phnom Penh, Two Parrots operates a specialized pepper and agri-food processing facility designed to meet international food safety standards.

  • Combined Capacity: 3,600 MT Black Pepper & 1,800 MT White Pepper annually.
  • Sterilization: Advanced steam-sterilization system ensuring product safety for international markets.
  • Quality Management System: Internal Control System (ICS) complying with GGAP, GAP, SRP, and Organic standards.
  • Certifications: GMP, HACCP, Global GAP, FDA registered, USDA Organic certified.

Quality Management System (QMS)

Our internal quality assurance team and external experts handle rigorous monitoring at every stage of the processing line.

Testing Protocol: Comprehensive Microbiological, Chemical, and Physical testing before export.

About Two Parrots Enterprise

Established in 2009 and headquartered in Phnom Penh, Two Parrots Enterprise is a growing Cambodian agri-food business dedicated to producing, processing, and distributing high-quality spices, rice-based flours, and instant food products. In 2025, Two Parrots Trading Solutions Co., Ltd. was incorporated to scale export operations worldwide.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, high-quality, and affordable Cambodian food products that enrich the daily lives of our consumers while supporting local farmers and promoting sustainable agriculture.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and leading brand in the spice, rice flour, and instant food industry across Cambodia and beyond.

Core Values

Quality First

Commitment to maintaining high product standards and international food safety compliance.

Sustainability

Working closely with local farmers to ensure fair trade and environmentally friendly practices.

Innovation

Developing diverse, value-added products to meet modern consumer demands.

Trust & Integrity

Building long-term partnerships with customers, suppliers, and farming communities.

Development Partners & ESG

Two Parrots actively collaborates with international development partners including Australian Aid (CAPRED), European Union / GIZ, USAID / Harvest3, and the Asian Development Bank (ADB Frontier) to upgrade smallholder value chains and help farmers secure Global G.A.P. international certifications.
